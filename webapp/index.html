What is Angel Arena?
Angel Arena is an AI-powered creative studio inside Telegram. Upload a photo, describe what you want, and let AI generate images or videos. All processing runs on high-end GPUs (NVIDIA H100) for maximum quality and speed.
How do tokens work?
Each generation costs tokens:
• Image (Inpaint / Edit / Generate) — 1 token
• Video — 37 tokens

Token packages:
• 10 tokens — 100 ⭐
• 30 tokens — 250 ⭐
• 100 tokens — 700 ⭐

Premium users get unlimited generations.
What modes are available?
Inpaint — Mask & replace parts of an image. Draw over the area you want to change, describe the result.

Video — Animate a photo into a multi-scene NSFW video using AI. Supports 6 scenes with customizable prompts and LoRAs.

Edit Easy — Quick AI edit with flexible strength. Good for subtle modifications.

Edit Dark — Advanced edit with own model. Best for face swaps, style changes, and high-detail work.

Generate — Create images from text only. No photo needed.
How to generate a video?
1. Switch to Video mode
2. Upload a source photo
3. Pick a preset (Missionary, Blowjob, etc.) or write your own prompt for each scene
4. Adjust LoRAs per scene for action style (⚙️ button)
5. Hit Generate

Generation takes ~5 minutes. The result is a multi-scene video (6 scenes, ~15 seconds at 32 FPS).
What are LoRAs?
LoRAs are small AI models trained to produce specific actions or styles. Each scene has 4 HIGH and 4 LOW LoRA slots. The first two are pre-filled with quality LoRAs (DR34ML4Y + NSFW-22). Use the ⚙️ icon to change them per scene.

Tip: Match your LoRA to your prompt. For example, use "Missionary" LoRAs with a missionary prompt for best results.

Tips for best results
• Use high-quality photos with good lighting
• For video, portrait orientation works best
• Be specific in prompts — describe the action, camera angle, and details
• For face consistency, use a face LoRA trained on your character
• LoRA strength 0.6–0.9 is optimal. Too high = artifacts, too low = no effect
• The first 2 scenes set the motion direction, later scenes follow
