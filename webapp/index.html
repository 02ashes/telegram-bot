What is Angel Arena?
Angel Arena is an AI-powered creative studio inside Telegram. Upload a photo, describe what you want, and let AI generate images or videos. All processing runs on high-end GPUs (NVIDIA H100) for maximum quality and speed.
How do tokens work?
Each generation costs tokens:
• Image — 1 token
• Video — 37 tokens

Token packages:
• 10 tokens — 100 ⭐
• 30 tokens — 250 ⭐
• 100 tokens — 700 ⭐

Premium users get unlimited generations.
What modes are available?
Inpaint — Mask & replace parts of an image.

Video — Animate a photo into a multi-scene video.

Edit Easy — Quick AI edit with flexible strength.

Edit Dark — Advanced edit with own model.

Generate — Create images from text only.
What is Premium?
Premium gives you:
• Unlimited generations
• Priority queue
• Access to all features

Price: 1500 ⭐ for 30 days.
Tips for best results
• Use high-quality photos with good lighting
• Be specific in prompts
• LoRA strength 0.6–0.9 is optimal
